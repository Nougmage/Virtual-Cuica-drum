Motion Friction Mechanics

Rapid back-and-forth cursor movements simulate pushing and pulling the wet dampener cloth along the internal bamboo stick (*gambiarra*). Reversing stroke direction causes instant pitch dips and noise bursts, mimicking physical stick friction.

How to Play & Scratch

Scratching Motion

Click and drag in rapid back-and-forth strokes (up/down, left/right, diagonal). Reversing direction creates natural push/pull friction timbre changes.

Continuous Pitch Control

Drag outwards towards the rim to simulate applying heavy thumb pressure (bending pitch up to +2 octaves). Dragging near the center yields deep open groans.

Keyboard Controls

  • Space / J: High-pressure squeak.
  • Down / K: Low-pressure deep groan.
  • A / D (Left/Right): Smooth pitch slide down/up.
